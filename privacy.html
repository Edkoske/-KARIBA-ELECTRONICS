Privacy Notice

How Kariba Electronics handles customer information.

On this pageInformation we collectHow we use itSharingYour choicesContact

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Kariba Electronics respects your privacy. This notice explains how information supplied through this website, email, phone or WhatsApp may be used to respond to you and provide customer service.

1. Information we collect

We may receive your name, phone number, email address, delivery details, product interests and the contents of your message when you contact us. The website may also process basic technical information needed to display pages and protect the service.

2. How we use information

  • To answer product, delivery and support enquiries.
  • To confirm orders, payment arrangements and delivery details.
  • To provide warranty or after-sales assistance.
  • To improve product information and website usability.
  • To meet legal, accounting or fraud-prevention requirements.

3. When information is shared

We may share relevant information with delivery providers, manufacturers or service partners only when needed to fulfil an order, provide support or meet a legal obligation. We do not sell customer contact information.

4. Retention and security

We keep information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose collected, customer support, records or legal requirements. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please do not send passwords, PINs or sensitive payment credentials through forms or chat.

5. Your choices

You may ask what personal information we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate information, or ask us to stop non-essential communications. Some records may need to be retained where required for business or legal purposes.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, email info@karibaelectronics.co.ke or call 0729725614.

This notice should be reviewed against the business's actual data practices and applicable Kenyan privacy requirements before launch.
