Shipping & Returns

What to expect after your order is confirmed.

On this pageDeliveryReceiving an orderReturns and exchangesWarrantyContact

Last updated: 29 September 2026

1. Delivery

We arrange delivery across Kenya where available. Delivery charges and estimated timelines vary by destination, product size, stock status and the selected delivery method. The final details are confirmed before dispatch.

Please provide a reachable phone number and accurate delivery information. Delays may occur because of weather, transport disruption, supplier delays or circumstances outside our reasonable control.

2. Receiving your order

Where practical, inspect the package before accepting it. If packaging appears damaged or the wrong item is delivered, note the issue with the delivery representative and contact us as soon as possible with your order details and photographs.

3. Returns and exchanges

Contact us before sending any item back. Eligibility depends on the product, its condition and the reason for return. Items should normally be unused, complete with accessories and packaging, and accompanied by proof of purchase. Some opened, installed, personalised, hygiene-sensitive or consumable products may not be eligible for change-of-mind returns.

Approved returns are assessed before a refund, repair or exchange is arranged. Delivery or handling charges may not be refundable unless the item is faulty, incorrect or otherwise covered by an applicable consumer protection requirement.

4. Warranty and faults

Warranty terms differ by manufacturer and product. If a product develops a fault, contact us with the proof of purchase and a description of the issue. We may troubleshoot, inspect, repair, replace or refer the item to the authorised service provider according to the applicable warranty.

5. Refunds

Where a refund is approved, we will confirm the method and expected processing time. Refund timing can depend on the payment provider or bank. We do not request card PINs or one-time passwords to process a refund.

6. Contact

For delivery, return or warranty support, call 0729725614, WhatsApp us, or email info@karibaelectronics.co.ke.

Please confirm the product-specific return window, warranty provider and delivery charge at the time of purchase. This page does not replace rights that apply under mandatory consumer protection law.
