Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you place an enquiry or order.

TopicsOrdersPaymentDeliverySupportContact

How do I place an order?

Choose a product, then use the WhatsApp or Gmail enquiry button. We will confirm availability, final pricing, delivery and payment details before accepting the order.

Are the prices on the website final?

Product listings are for guidance and enquiry. Stock and prices can change, so we confirm the final amount before dispatch.

Which payment methods are available?

Payment options depend on the order and are confirmed directly with the customer. Never share your M-Pesa PIN, card PIN or one-time password with anyone claiming to represent Kariba Electronics.

Do you deliver outside Nairobi?

We support delivery across Kenya where available. Charges and timelines depend on your location, product and delivery method. Contact us to confirm before ordering.

Can I collect an item?

Collection availability depends on the current operating location and stock. Please confirm collection details with the team before travelling.

How does warranty support work?

Warranty coverage depends on the manufacturer and product. Keep your proof of purchase and contact us promptly if you experience a fault. See our Shipping & Returns Policy for more information.

How do I contact the team?

Call or WhatsApp 0729725614, or email info@karibaelectronics.co.ke. Opening hours are Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Sunday, 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

Where can I read your policies?

See our Terms & Conditions, Privacy Notice, and Shipping & Returns pages for the full customer information.
